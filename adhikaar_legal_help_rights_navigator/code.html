Landlord Refusing to Return Security Deposit

Official procedural pathway when tenancy has ended, premises are surrendered, but security money is unjustly withheld or arbitrary renovation fees are charged.

download Get Notice Template
menu_book Governing Law & Citations

Section 108(m), Transfer of Property Act, 1882 & Model Tenancy Act, Section 13(2)

The lessee is bound to keep the property in as good condition as it was at the time when he was put into possession, subject only to the changes caused by reasonable wear and tear or irresistible force.

1

Pre-Agreed Deductions Only

A landlord cannot unilaterally invent deductions. Any repair charge must correspond to verifiable damage beyond ordinary wear-and-tear, backed by itemized tax invoices.

2

Strict 30-Day Limit

Under modern state tenancy regulations, security deposits must be refunded within thirty days of tenant handover. Continued non-payment creates liability for commercial bank interest.

3

Unfair Trade Practice

If the property is rented via institutional brokers or co-living corporations, withholding deposit constitutes an actionable “Deficiency in Service” under Consumer Protection Act 2019.

What counts as "Normal Wear & Tear"?

Legally protected versus legitimate landlord deductions

info
check_circle Tenant Protected (No Deduction Permitted)
  • • Faded wall paint from normal sun exposure or age
  • • Minor scuffs or microscopic nail holes from hanging photo frames
  • • Tap washers worn out by municipal hard water
cancel Permissible Deductions (If itemized)
  • • Deep unauthorized wall cracks, burns, or large unpatched anchor bolts
  • • Broken ceramic sanitary ware or cracked marble slabs
  • • Unpaid electricity, water, or monthly society maintenance dues
01

Dispatch Formal Demand Notice via Registered Post AD

15-Day Cure Period

Never rely solely on phone calls. Issue a signed demand notice explicitly stating the key handover date, deposit balance, and 15 days deadline. Send via Registered Post with Acknowledgment Due (RPAD) or Speed Post to generate a tamper-proof court record.

02

Lodge E-Daakhil Complaint or File with Rent Authority

No Lawyer Mandatory

If the landlord fails to remit the amount after 15 days of notice receipt, submit an online complaint via e-Daakhil (Consumer Commission) or the designated District Rent Authority (under the State Tenancy Act).

open_in_new Portal: edaakhil.nic.in Statutory filing fee: ₹0 for claims up to ₹5,00,000
03

Pre-Litigation Lok Adalat Settlement

Zero Court Fee & Same-Day Decree

Request a Pre-Litigation Lok Adalat hearing via your District Legal Services Authority (DLSA). A neutral judicial officer and conciliator will summon the landlord for a settlement. Any signed agreement carries the exact weight of a civil court decree without appealable delay.

District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) Helpdesk

Every district in India has a DLSA housed within the District Court complex. If your income falls under state threshold limits (typically under ₹3,00,000/yr), or under specific vulnerable categories, you are entitled to a court-appointed legal aid advocate at zero fee.
