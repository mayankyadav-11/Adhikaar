Statutory Protection Wages Must Be Cleared by 7th–10th

Employer cannot withhold earned compensation beyond statutory cycle without cause.

3-Step Action Pathway

1

Formal Demand Notice

Send a registered email/speed post giving 15 days to clear pending dues under Section 15.

2

File with Labour Commissioner (ALC)

Submit Form A claim with salary slips, offer letter, and bank statements at district labour court.

3

Compensation Award

Court can order payment of dues plus statutory compensation up to 10x the delayed amount.
